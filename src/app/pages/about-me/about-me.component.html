Vicenza, Italy · 2005

biography and profile

A life in motion,
grounded in craft.

Born in Vicenza, Italy, Paolo began dancing at ten, discovering in ballet a language for both precision and freedom.

His journey began at Domus Danza School in Schio under Enrica Marcucci. In 2020, he joined the Annarella Sanchez International Conservatory, where he met pas de deux partner Nuria Fernandes. Together they won Youth America Grand Prix honours for three consecutive years.

In 2024, after winning the YAGP semi-finals, Paolo qualified for the United States finals. His performance of the Don Quixote variation placed him in the Top 12 of the Senior Men category. Now working at Brno Theatre, he continues to deepen his technique, broaden his artistic voice, and build a career defined by generosity, courage, and curiosity.
